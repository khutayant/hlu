## Notes

- **Sidebar menu** = app left sider (Locations, Category, Programs, …).
- **Menu panel** = left nav **inside** the content area (e.g. Locations → Country / Township; Programs → Programs / Batches / Doses). Not the app sidebar.
- When the user says **menu panel**, build the in-content left panel pattern (same shell as user-role detail / Locations), not a nested sidebar submenu.
